Retro terminal text-to-speech synthesizer inspired by the "I Feel Fantastic" video and 1980s speech synthesis technology.

Combines two TTS engines — SAM (Software Automatic Mouth, 1982) and macOS system voices — with a real-time Web Audio effects chain: filter, LFO, delay, drone, and a "sing mode" that recreates the eerie tonal quality of early robotic speech.

Type text, press Enter, twist the knobs.

ENGINES: SAM 1982 · macOS Say

EFFECTS: Filter · LFO · Delay · Drone

MODES: Sing · Loop · Voice Cycling
